Method. Every OpenStreetMap land-use location in the Langat corridor is measured by its perpendicular distance to the nearest watercourse (river, canal or drain). Scope is limited to the three districts that form the Langat River Basin — Hulu Langat, Sepang and Kuala Langat — and only locations inside the 1.5 km riparian zone are listed here. The risk score combines a category's typical pollutant load with its proximity to a watercourse — it is not a measurement of actual discharge.

How WQI Is Calculated

The official DOE Malaysia formula — implemented in full in this portal

Weighted formula

Six sub-indices combined at fixed weights
WQI = 0.22·SIDO + 0.19·SIBOD + 0.16·SICOD
        + 0.15·SINH₃-N + 0.16·SISS + 0.12·SIpH
Each sub-index (SI) converts a raw reading to a 0–100 scale through the DOE best-fit equations. Dissolved oxygen is normalised to percent saturation before being sub-indexed. The final 0–100 value then maps onto the five classes above.

Data provenance notice. River geometry, water bodies, district boundaries, land-use locations and the national basin statistics are real data from the sources listed above. The parameter readings at the 16 monitoring stations are sample data, generated along a plausible upstream-to-downstream profile purely to demonstrate the portal. They are not official readings and must not be used for decisions. data/stations.json defines the schema to be filled with actual LUAS/DOE monitoring data.
