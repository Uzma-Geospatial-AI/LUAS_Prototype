Earth surface activity SESAMS

Selangor Earth Surface Activity Monitoring System — the application MYSA (Agensi Angkasa Malaysia) built for LUAS to monitor land-use activity, above all on and around water bodies and wherever it could put a load into the state's water. Here it runs on the Langat catchment: every mapped land-use site, how close it sits to water, whether it holds a licence, and the quarterly satellite view of the water beside it.
