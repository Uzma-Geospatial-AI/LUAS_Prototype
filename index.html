Phase 3

LEDS · TMDL · SESAMS — load accounting

The Total Maximum Daily Load is the mass of a pollutant this reach can receive per day and still hold its class. Set against what the river already carries and what has been licensed through SESAMS, it answers the operational question: how much discharge is left to licence?

TMDL = ΣWLA + ΣLA + MOSloading capacity = licensed point sources + background & non-point + margin of safety

Design conditions

Sets the ambient standard the capacity is computed against.
Reserved capacity covering model and measurement uncertainty.
EQ (Industrial Effluent) Regulations 2009.
Design flow is an unverified estimate. A TMDL must be written for a low-flow design condition (MAM7 or 7Q10), when the river has least capacity to assimilate a load. Replace this value with the DID gauged record for Sungai Langat at Dengkil, then tick Verified. Every load figure on this page scales directly with it.

Add or Edit a Licence

The load this licence would add, and whether it fits the remaining capacity
Permitted concentration
Method. River load = concentration (mg/L) × design flow (m³/s) × 86.4. Licence wasteload = permitted concentration (mg/L) × permitted flow (m³/day) ÷ 1000. The in-river concentration is the 12-month median at the Phase 1 station, which is steadier than any single sample. Diffuse load is whatever the river carries that licensed point sources do not account for — background, run-off and unlicensed discharge. Remaining capacity = TMDL − MOS − current in-river load.
